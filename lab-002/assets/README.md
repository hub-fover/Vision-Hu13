# Assets

Only real captured media or real device recordings may be added here. Record
their source license and modifications in the LAB 002 asset manifest.

- `asset-manifest.json` is the machine-readable provenance ledger.
- `SOURCES.md` is the human-readable source and modification record.
- `samples/` contains the canonical JPEG-90 derivatives used by validation.

The samples remain Pexels-licensed third-party content. They are not part of
the project's CC BY 4.0 original-content license.
